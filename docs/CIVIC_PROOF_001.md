# Civic Proof 001 — Turn a neighborhood idea into an official action path

Date checked: 2026-09-19

## Real-world need

> I have an idea that could improve my neighborhood, but I do not know whether there is public funding or where to start.

This is intentionally non-partisan. COMMONS does not choose the citizen's position or project idea.

## Official opportunity found

**Gebietsfonds Helle Mitte**

Official Berlin source:
https://www.berlin.de/ba-marzahn-hellersdorf/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1713001.php

The official announcement says:

- local actors in Helle Mitte can apply for financial support;
- eligible actors include businesses, property owners, private individuals, associations and institutions;
- the fund can cover up to 50% of eligible project costs;
- EUR 10,000 is available in each of 2026 and 2027;
- ideas can be submitted until 25 October 2026;
- examples include local public-space improvements, art or planting actions, neighborhood events, sports events and similar local projects.

## COMMONS action plan

1. Verify the project is inside the Helle Mitte funding area.
2. Ask the citizen for their own project goal in plain language.
3. Check the proposal against the official eligibility criteria.
4. Identify missing information, budget items and evidence.
5. Draft the application in the citizen's own stated terms.
6. Citizen reviews and approves every substantive claim.
7. Submit only through the official channel.
8. Store the submission receipt / confirmation as proof.
9. Later store the authority's response.
10. If funded and completed, record the observable outcome.

## Proof status

**Stage: source_verified**

External proof currently available:
- official Berlin announcement;
- current public deadline;
- responsible public body and program.

What is **not yet proven**:
- a real citizen used COMMONS;
- an application was submitted;
- the authority acknowledged it;
- funding was awarded;
- a neighborhood outcome occurred.

The test is only successful when these stages are kept separate.

## Why this matters

The value is not political persuasion. It is reducing the friction between:

**citizen intention → legitimate public channel → completed action → verifiable response**

That is a concrete form of civic capability.
