# WORLD PULSE v3 — LIVING ATLAS

## North star

**A living atlas of what changes — and what humans do next.**

WORLD PULSE should not look like a dashboard with a globe behind it. It should behave like a planetary instrument whose visual language carries meaning before a person reads the copy.

## Signature grammar

### ◉ Pulse
A sourced disturbance or signal.
A pulse says: **something changed**.
It does **not** say how many people were harmed, what caused the event, or what intervention is appropriate.

### — Thread
A verified response pathway.
A thread says: **accountable human action exists**.
The visual path is a semantic device. Unless an authoritative logistics source exists, it must never be presented as a tracked shipment, travel route, causal chain, or literal movement of aid.

### ✦ Bloom
Documented improvement or response evidence.
A bloom says: **a dated source supports a concrete change in the world**.
It must not appear merely because an intervention was announced.

## Earth direction
The stock photographic Earth texture is deliberately removed.
The planet is rendered as:
- black / obsidian sphere,
- restrained graticule,
- mineral land geometry from a public world atlas,
- faint country edges,
- semantic event layers above the surface.

The goal is closer to a museum instrument or illuminated atlas than a game map.

## Spatial composition system
Story scenes are not repeated cards.
Each scene selects a composition:
- left-monument
- metric-left
- right-whisper
- center-monument
- low-left
- metric-right
- final-center

The composition controls text position, globe offset and narrative scale.

## Time as an instrument
The bottom rail is not a progress indicator. It is a time control:
1. **26 Aug — Pulse**
2. **27 Aug — Thread**
3. **08 Sep — Bloom**
4. **Now — Open loop**

Dragging it changes both the story scene and the physical state of the atlas.

## Nepal story
### Pulse
Flash floods strike northern Nepal.
### Scale
~93,000 people may have been affected, clearly labeled as an early estimate.
### Thread
A formal IFRC Emergency Appeal and Nepal Red Cross Society response are verified.
### Response
CHF 25M Emergency Appeal and named response priorities.
### Bloom
IFRC reports safe drinking water restored for around 2,000 people in Nuwakot.
### Capacity
Mobile primary clinic capacity: 100 patients/day.
### Meaning
Pulse → Thread → Bloom becomes legible as a reusable public grammar.
### Open loop
The viewer can help, inspect evidence, or pass the story on.

## Language direction
Avoid software-dashboard wording when a more human phrase is clearer.
- Explore Earth → **Look around**
- Evidence → **Why we believe this**
- Share → **Pass this on**
- Watch Action 001 → **Enter Nepal**

## Truth contract
Art direction never overrides epistemic discipline.
- Never fake live.
- Never turn an early estimate into a final count.
- Never infer human impact from physical event magnitude alone.
- Never show a funding percentage without a current authoritative source.
- Never imply an individual contribution caused an outcome without causal evidence.
- Never let a semantic response thread look like a tracked logistics route without saying what it represents.
- Keep historical claims dated instead of silently rewriting them.
- Unresolved operations remain open loops.

## Share contract
A shared artifact should preserve:
- place,
- event,
- dated outcome,
- loop state,
- Pulse / Thread / Bloom grammar,
- route back to the interactive evidence chain.

## Success test
A screenshot should be recognizable as WORLD PULSE even if the logo is removed.
A new visitor should understand, without reading documentation:
1. something happened,
2. humans responded,
3. there is evidence of what changed,
4. the evidence can be inspected,
5. the story is not finished.
