# COMMONS Calm Instrument v1

## Goal

Make WORLD PULSE habitable enough to check daily without weakening its auditability.

The product should answer two different user needs:

1. **Orientation:** What matters right now? Can I rely on this state?
2. **Audit:** Show me the claims, sources, freshness rules, evaluations and incidents.

Those needs should not compete for the same visual density.

## Home orientation

The home surface now computes a compact orientation from existing product state:

- stories tracked
- stories with documented positive outcome state
- verified direct action paths that currently pass trust gates
- stale critical claims

These values are derived from the story catalog, Action Loop catalog and Trust report. They are not hand-written counters.

## Progressive disclosure

Trust Center has two modes.

### Simple

Default for normal use.

It explains:

- current trust state
- provenance coverage
- stale critical evidence
- unresolved conflicts
- evaluation pass state
- the three most important claims for the current story
- known limitations

The goal is to answer: **Can I rely on this right now, and why?**

### Audit

Full inspection mode.

It preserves:

- complete Claim Ledger
- Source Registry
- evaluations
- incidents
- capability limits
- raw trust registry access

The goal is to answer: **Show me everything.**

## Inspection choreography

Opening Trust during a cinematic story is treated as a mode transition, not a drawer placed on top of content.

The story:

- yields horizontal space
- reduces visual dominance
- shifts the globe
- quiets story chrome

The Trust Center becomes the dominant inspection surface while the underlying story remains spatially present.

Simple mode uses a narrower panel than Audit mode.

## Readability

Audit mode keeps full detail but increases claim typography, spacing and limitation visibility.

Repeated boxed cards are reduced in favor of a cleaner ledger rhythm.

## Product rule

Calm does not mean hiding uncertainty.

It means:

> show the minimum needed to orient safely, then make the full proof easy to inspect.

## Non-goals

Calm Instrument v1 does not:

- claim that the atlas covers all important world events
- create fake daily urgency
- optimize time-on-site
- hide degraded trust states
- remove the full audit path

A healthy daily session can still end in under a minute.
