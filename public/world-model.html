TRUST CONTRACT

Models may disagree. COMMONS must show that.

Not a probability

The first Forecast Council compares deterministic model outputs. “High agreement” means low spread, not a calibrated 80% or 90% chance.

History is not destiny

Weather analogues compare physical variables. Similar rainfall does not imply similar damage, exposure or response.

Flood guidance has limits

GloFAS is modelled river discharge at kilometre scale. It may represent a nearby major river rather than the exact local waterway.

Backtest everything

Forecast snapshots are archived so future COMMONS can compare what models said with what actually happened.
