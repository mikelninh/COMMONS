BUSINESS RULES

Trust is part of the business model.

No emergency paywall

Core public-interest information remains accessible.

No ads

Attention incentives would directly conflict with calm, calibrated orientation.

No user-data sale

Revenue should come from professional capability, not surveillance.

Commercial data compliance

Before charging, switch every provider that requires it to a commercial licence or direct open-data pipeline.

FIRST REVENUE MILESTONE

Three teams paying for ten watchpoints each.

That is enough to validate the paid job before we build accounts, billing, enterprise dashboards or a giant sales machine.
