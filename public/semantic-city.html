The Heat scenario joins four official Berlin source families: Klimaanalysekarten 2022, Umweltgerechtigkeit 2023/2024, Grünanlagenbestand and the hospital dataset. These sources have different reference dates and purposes; asset proximity is not live service availability, and structural climate evidence is not current weather.
